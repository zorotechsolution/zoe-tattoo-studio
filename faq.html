Tattoo

Everything about custom designs, consultations, appointments, and getting inked.

Before getting your first tattoo, take time to consider your preferred art style (fine-line, realism, traditional, tribal, or blackwork), the placement on your body, size, and long-term significance. We encourage booking a consultation with our resident artists to discuss your ideas, skin tone compatibility, and how the tattoo will naturally age and flow with your body anatomy.

Yes, absolutely! You are welcome to bring sketches, reference images, Pinterest boards, or personal concepts. Our artists will collaborate directly with you to refine, resize, and custom-tailor the design so it fits your aesthetic and anatomical placement perfectly.

Tattoo pricing depends on several factors: design complexity, size, placement on the body, color requirements, and the estimated time needed to complete the piece. Smaller flash pieces and minimal fine-line tattoos have fixed upfront rates, while extensive multi-session custom work (like full sleeves or large back pieces) is billed at an hourly rate or project package. We provide clear, transparent quotes during your consultation.

Pain varies from person to person. Generally, areas with thicker skin, muscle, and fat (such as outer arms, forearms, shoulders, and outer thighs) cause milder discomfort. Sensitive zones with thin skin or dense nerve endings over bone (such as ribs, spine, collarbone, fingers, inner wrist, ankles, and neck) are more intense. Our artists maintain a gentle touch, offer comfort breaks, and ensure you feel relaxed throughout your session.

To ensure the smoothest session and best ink retention:

  • Get a good night’s sleep before your session.
  • Eat a substantial meal 1–2 hours prior and stay well-hydrated to keep blood sugar stable.
  • Avoid alcohol, blood thinners, and aspirin for at least 24 hours before your appointment.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing that gives easy access to the tattoo placement area.
  • Avoid heavy sun exposure or tanning on the area leading up to your date.

After Care

Crucial healing guidelines, daily cleaning steps, and protection for tattoos, piercings, and laser treatments.

If your artist applied a protective medical adhesive film (like Saniderm or Dermalize), leave it on for 3 to 5 days as directed. If wrapped in traditional cling film, remove it after 2 to 4 hours. Gently wash the tattoo with lukewarm water and a mild, fragrance-free antibacterial liquid soap using clean hands. Gently pat dry with a clean paper towel—never rub. Allow it to air-dry for 10 minutes before applying a very thin layer of recommended aftercare ointment.

For the first 2 to 3 weeks:

  • Wash: Clean the tattoo 2 to 3 times daily with mild antibacterial fragrance-free soap.
  • Dry: Gently pat dry with clean disposable paper towels.
  • Moisturize: Apply a tiny, breathable layer of tattoo aftercare balm or unscented lotion (e.g., Aquaphor, Hustle Butter, or Lubriderm). Avoid heavy smothered layers, as the skin needs air to heal.
  • Maintain: Continue daily moisturizing for 3 to 4 weeks until the skin feels smooth and completely renewed.

Yes, completely normal! Around day 4 to 7, the top layer of skin will begin to peel and flake like a mild sunburn, which can cause itching. Do not scratch, pick, or peel the flakes or scabs, as doing so can pull ink out of the dermis and cause light spots or scarring. Lightly pat the area or apply a dab of lotion to soothe irritation.

To avoid infection and pigment loss during the 3–4 week healing window:

  • No soaking: Do not submerge the tattoo in swimming pools, hot tubs, baths, lakes, or the ocean. Quick showers are fine.
  • No sun exposure: Keep the healing skin out of direct sunlight and tanning beds. Apply SPF 50+ only after it has fully healed.
  • Avoid friction: Do not wear tight or restrictive clothing that rubs against the fresh tattoo.
  • Limit intense workouts: Avoid heavy gym sessions, stretching the area, or excessive sweating for the first 3 to 5 days.

Clean your piercing twice daily using a sterile 0.9% saline wound wash spray (such as NeilMed). Spray directly on the front and back of the piercing, then gently pat dry with disposable sterile gauze or a paper towel. Never twist, turn, or push the jewelry. Avoid rubbing alcohol, hydrogen peroxide, Dettol, tea tree oil, or heavy ointments, which severely dry out and damage healing tissue.

Following laser treatment, keep the treated area clean, dry, and protected for the first 48 hours. Apply a thin layer of soothing post-laser ointment (or pure aloe) and a sterile dressing if needed. Cool ice compresses wrapped in a clean cloth can help alleviate swelling and heat. Never pop or pick blisters or crusts. Protect the treated area with broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen once healed.

Normal healing includes mild redness, localized swelling, warmness, tenderness, and slight clear or pale lymph discharge in the first few days. Signs of an infection include expanding bright redness, intense throbbing pain that worsens after day 3, thick yellow/green foul-smelling pus, red streaks spreading from the site, or a fever. If you suspect an infection, reach out to us and consult a physician promptly.

Tattoo Removal

Everything you need to know about PicoSure® laser technology, sessions, pain management, and results.

Most clients compare the feeling of laser tattoo removal to a rubber band snapping against the skin. At ZOE Studio, we utilize state-of-the-art continuous skin cryo-chilling systems throughout your treatment and provide topical numbing cream options to ensure your session is as comfortable and manageable as possible.

On average, most tattoos require between 5 to 10 sessions for complete clearance. The exact number depends on the tattoo's size, ink density, ink depth, pigment colors, tattoo age, and your body's metabolic rate. During your free consultation, our certified laser specialists provide a realistic, customized session estimate tailored to your tattoo.

Yes. Black and dark blue pigments respond fastest because they absorb laser energy across all wavelengths. Challenging colors like greens, vibrant blues, reds, and yellows require specialized wavelengths. Our advanced PicoSure® and dual picosecond/nanosecond systems deliver targeted pulses that break down even the most stubborn multi-colored pigments effectively.

When performed by certified professionals with modern picosecond laser technology, the risk of scarring is under 1%. Picosecond lasers shatter ink particles using acoustic pressure waves rather than excessive thermal heat, protecting surrounding tissue and preserving the natural texture of your skin.

We recommend waiting 6 to 8 weeks between laser sessions. The laser fractures ink into microscopic dust, but your body's immune and lymphatic systems need time to naturally filter and flush those particles away. Adequate spacing prevents skin irritation and yields maximum ink clearance per treatment.

Yes, cosmetic tattoos (including eyebrow microblading, lip blush, and eyeliner) can be safely removed or lightened. Because cosmetic pigments often contain iron oxide formulations that can briefly oxidize during laser exposure, we perform a test patch and utilize specialized low-fluence settings tailored for delicate facial skin.

Laser cover-up fading only requires 2 to 4 sessions to significantly lighten dark, heavy ink rather than achieving 100% removal. This creates a soft, neutral canvas that allows your tattoo artist to design a vibrant, intricate new tattoo without having to use dense black shapes or dark colors to hide the old piece.

We treat all skin tones and Fitzpatrick types safely. For melanin-rich skin, we utilize longer wavelengths (such as 1064nm Nd:YAG) which bypass surface epidermal melanin and specifically target deep ink deposits. This eliminates the risk of hyperpigmentation or hypopigmentation (light spots).

Body Piercing

Details about piercing services, sterile needle techniques, jewelry materials, and healing times.

We provide a comprehensive selection of professional body piercings:

  • Ear Piercings: Standard & upper lobe, helix, forward helix, tragus, anti-tragus, conch, daith, rook, snug, and industrial.
  • Facial & Nose: Nostril, high nostril, septum, bridge, and eyebrow.
  • Oral & Lip: Medusa (philtrum), Monroe, labret, vertical labret, snake bites, and tongue.
  • Body & Surface: Navel (belly button), nipple piercings, and microdermal anchors.

We strictly use 100% single-use, sterile, surgical-grade hollow piercing needles—we NEVER use piercing guns. Needles slice cleanly through tissue with precision and minimal trauma, promoting fast, comfortable healing. Piercing guns force blunt jewelry through skin, can shatter cartilage, and cannot be properly autoclave-sterilized.

We exclusively pierce with certified Implant-Grade Titanium (ASTM F-136), 14k/18k solid gold, and implant-grade surgical steel. Implant-grade titanium is 100% hypoallergenic, nickel-free, lightweight, and completely biocompatible—making it the gold standard for quick healing and zero allergic reactions.

Healing timelines depend on blood circulation and cartilage density:

  • Earlobes: 6 to 8 weeks
  • Ear Cartilage (Helix, Tragus, Conch, Daith): 6 to 12 months
  • Septum: 2 to 3 months
  • Nostril: 4 to 6 months
  • Navel (Belly Button): 6 to 9 months
  • Lip / Tongue: 6 to 8 weeks

The sensation is very brief—usually lasting just a couple of seconds. Most clients describe it as a quick pinch and localized pressure. Earlobe and septum piercings are minimal in pain, while cartilage or dense tissue may feel slightly sharper. Our professional piercers use steady breathing techniques to make your experience swift, smooth, and relaxing.

For health, safety, and hygiene standards, all initial piercings must be performed using our in-studio, pre-sterilized, implant-grade titanium jewelry with appropriate post length to accommodate swelling. Once your piercing is fully healed, you are welcome to bring in your own jewelry, and our piercers will happily assist you with safe insertion.

Piercing bumps are almost always irritation bumps caused by accidental friction, sleeping on the piercing, touching/twisting jewelry, or using improper cleaning solutions. Continue spraying with sterile saline twice daily, keep the area dry, and avoid sleeping on that side. If the bump persists, visit our studio for a free jewelry assessment and downsize check.

Initial jewelry is intentionally fitted with a slightly longer post to accommodate early swelling. Once swelling subsides (usually between 4 to 8 weeks depending on the piercing), it is important to return to our studio to downsize to a shorter, flush-fitting post. Downsizing prevents snagging, irritation bumps, and angle migration.
