Tailored Solutions for Every Tattoo

We offer a comprehensive range of tattoo removal treatments designed to address every type of ink, skin tone, and personal goal. Each service is customized to deliver the safest, most effective results possible.

PicoSure Laser Removal

The gold standard in laser tattoo removal, PicoSure technology delivers ultra-short picosecond pulses of energy to target ink particles with unprecedented precision. Unlike traditional lasers that rely on heat to break down ink, PicoSure uses photomechanical pressure to shatter even the most stubborn pigment into microscopic fragments that your body naturally eliminates. This means faster visible results, fewer total sessions, and significantly less damage to surrounding tissue. Whether your tattoo is a single-color piece or a complex multi-shaded design, PicoSure adapts to deliver optimal clearance with minimal discomfort and downtime.

Picosecond + Nanosecond Dual

For the most challenging and multi-colored tattoos, our dual-wavelength approach combines the power of both picosecond and nanosecond laser technology in a single treatment plan. The nanosecond wavelength targets larger ink particles and deeper pigment deposits, while the picosecond wavelength shatters the finer remaining fragments that traditional lasers leave behind. This synergistic combination dramatically accelerates the removal process for tattoos that have proven resistant to single-wavelength treatments. It is especially effective on professional tattoos with dense, saturated ink and vibrant colors like blues, greens, and purples that are notoriously difficult to remove.

Partial / Faded Cover-Up

Not every tattoo needs to be completely removed — sometimes the best path forward is strategic lightening to prepare your skin for a beautiful new piece. Our partial and faded cover-up service uses carefully calibrated laser settings to selectively target specific areas of your existing tattoo, gradually reducing the ink density to create the perfect canvas for your next artist. We work closely with you and your tattoo artist to determine exactly which sections need lightening and by how much, ensuring we preserve the elements you want to keep while fading the rest to the ideal level for a seamless cover-up.

Removal by Tattoo Type

Not all tattoos are created equal — and neither is their removal. From delicate cosmetic pigments implanted in sensitive facial tissue to dense tribal blackwork saturated deep into the dermis, each tattoo category demands a distinct approach. At ZOE Studio, we have developed targeted protocols for every major tattoo type, combining the right laser wavelengths, pulse durations, and treatment strategies to achieve the best possible clearance for your specific ink.

Permanent Makeup Removal

Permanent makeup tattoos — including eyebrow microblading, lip blushing, and eyeliner tattoos — are applied to some of the most delicate and visible areas of the face. These pigments are typically implanted at a shallower depth than body tattoos, but the skin in these regions is thinner and far more sensitive, requiring an exceptionally cautious approach. At ZOE Studio, we use precisely calibrated low-fluence laser settings to gradually break down cosmetic pigments without causing scarring or discoloration in the surrounding facial tissue. Our specialists understand the unique chemistry of iron oxide and titanium dioxide-based pigments commonly used in permanent makeup, and we tailor every session to protect your natural beauty while achieving effective ink clearance.

Eyebrow microblading
Lip blushing
Eyeliner tattoos

Cosmetic Tattoo Removal

Cosmetic tattoos such as areola reconstruction tattoos, scar camouflage pigments, and skin tone blending tattoos serve deeply personal and often medical purposes — but circumstances change, and you may need them corrected or removed entirely. These specialized tattoos use flesh-toned and pastel pigments that can be particularly tricky to treat, as some cosmetic inks contain compounds that darken or oxidize when exposed to laser energy. Our team at ZOE Studio has extensive experience navigating this challenge, employing test-patch protocols and carefully sequenced wavelength selections to prevent paradoxical darkening. We approach every cosmetic tattoo with the empathy and precision it deserves, ensuring your comfort and confidence throughout the removal journey.

Areola tattoos
Scar camouflage

Amateur & Stick-and-Poke Removal

Hand-poked tattoos, prison tattoos, and DIY stick-and-poke pieces are among the most common tattoos people seek to remove — and fortunately, they are also among the most responsive to laser treatment. Amateur tattoos are typically applied with non-professional equipment that deposits ink at inconsistent depths and densities, which actually works in our favor during removal since the ink is often less saturated and more accessible to laser energy. However, the inks used in amateur tattoos are frequently non-standard, sometimes containing unconventional materials like pen ink or soot-based pigments that can behave unpredictably under laser treatment. At ZOE Studio, we account for these variables with thorough initial assessments and adaptable treatment plans that ensure safe, effective results no matter the ink's origin.

Multi-Color & Vibrant Tattoo Removal

Rainbow tattoos, watercolor pieces, and new school designs are breathtaking works of art — but their broad spectrum of vibrant inks presents one of the most complex challenges in laser removal. Each color absorbs laser energy at different wavelengths, meaning a single treatment session often requires multiple passes with different laser handpieces to address the full palette effectively. Blues and greens are notoriously stubborn, requiring specialized 694nm or 755nm wavelengths, while yellows, oranges, and reds respond to different frequency ranges entirely. At ZOE Studio, our dual-wavelength PicoSure system and expert color-mapping protocols allow us to systematically target every hue in your tattoo, building a customized wavelength sequence that addresses each shade for the most complete clearance achievable.

Watercolor pieces

Tribal & Blackwork Removal

Dense black ink tattoos — including tribal sleeves, Maori-inspired designs, and full blackout pieces — represent the most ink-heavy category in tattoo removal and demand both patience and precision. The sheer volume of pigment in these tattoos means that more ink particles must be shattered and cleared by the body's lymphatic system, which naturally extends the timeline for complete removal. However, black ink absorbs laser energy across virtually all wavelengths, making each individual session highly effective at fragmenting the pigment. At ZOE Studio, we develop extended treatment plans for tribal and blackwork pieces that balance aggressive ink clearance with adequate healing intervals, protecting your skin's integrity while steadily working toward the cleanest possible result session after session.

Traumatic Tattoo Removal

Traumatic tattoos are unlike any other type — they are not intentional body art but rather the result of accidents that embed foreign particles beneath the skin, such as road debris from a motorcycle crash, pencil lead marks from a childhood fall, or gravel and asphalt forced into a wound. These embedded pigments often contain materials that respond differently to laser energy than standard tattoo inks, including metallic fragments, carbon, and mineral compounds. At ZOE Studio, we conduct thorough evaluations using magnification and imaging to identify the nature and depth of the embedded material before designing a treatment protocol. Our gentle, incremental approach ensures that even deeply embedded particles are progressively broken down and absorbed by the body, helping you move past the physical and emotional reminders of an unwanted accident.
