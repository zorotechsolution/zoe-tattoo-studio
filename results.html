Know What to Expect

Results by Tattoo Type

Not all tattoos respond the same way to laser treatment. Explore realistic expectations based on your ink type, density, and technique so you can plan your removal journey with confidence.

Black Ink Tattoos

Black ink tattoos are the easiest to remove because black pigment absorbs all laser wavelengths efficiently, regardless of the specific wavelength used. Typical sessions range from 3–6 for amateur tattoos and 5–10 for professional work, depending on ink density and depth. Clients can expect a clearance rate of 95% or higher, making black ink the most responsive of any ink type. Most patients see significant fading after just the first few sessions, with near-complete removal achievable for the majority of cases.

Multi-Colored Tattoos

Multi-colored tattoos require multiple wavelengths per session because each color responds differently to laser energy. Blacks and dark tones fade first, followed by reds and oranges, while blues and greens typically take the longest to clear. Typical sessions range from 8–15, using a sequential clearance approach that targets one color group at a time. Patience is essential, but our multi-wavelength technology ensures every hue is addressed systematically for the best possible outcome.

Cosmetic & Permanent Makeup

Cosmetic tattoos and permanent makeup require delicate handling due to their placement on sensitive facial skin and the unique composition of the pigments used. Microblading pigments may darken initially before they begin to fade, which is a normal part of the process. Lower energy settings are used to protect the delicate skin while still achieving effective ink fragmentation. Typical sessions range from 3–6, and a test patch is always performed first to gauge how the pigment will respond.

Tribal & Blackwork

Tribal and blackwork tattoos feature dense ink saturation, which means more sessions are needed compared to standard black ink tattoos — but black pigment still responds well to all laser wavelengths. Full sleeves or large-scale blackwork may take 12–20+ sessions for significant clearance due to the sheer volume of ink deposited in the skin. The good news is that patients typically notice significant visible fading after each session, which helps maintain motivation throughout the process. A clearance rate of 90% or higher is achievable with consistent treatment.

Cover-Up Tattoos

Cover-up tattoos present a unique challenge because they involve double ink density — the original tattoo underneath plus the cover-up artwork on top. This requires patience and a strategic approach, as both layers of ink need to be treated for effective removal. Typical sessions range from 10–18, and it is common for the original ink to resurface as the cover-up layer fades first. Understanding this process upfront helps set realistic expectations and ensures you stay committed to achieving the clear skin you want.

Amateur / Stick-and-Poke

Amateur and stick-and-poke tattoos are among the easiest to remove due to their shallow, inconsistent ink placement by non-professional hands. The pigment is typically deposited at varying depths and with less density than professional tattoos, making it far more accessible to laser energy. Typical sessions range from just 2–5, and many clients see dramatic improvement after only one or two treatments. These tattoos are excellent candidates for full clearance, often achieving complete removal in a fraction of the time required for professional work.
