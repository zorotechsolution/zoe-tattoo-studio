Tattoo Knowledge

Deep Understanding of Tattoo Science

Effective tattoo removal begins with understanding the science behind the ink. At ZOE, our expertise in tattoo artistry, ink chemistry, and skin biology allows us to create personalized treatment plans that deliver safer, more precise, and more effective results.

Understanding Tattoo Ink

Tattoo ink is placed beneath the skin's surface into the dermis, where it becomes trapped within the skin's cells. Because these ink particles are too large for the body to naturally eliminate, tattoos can remain visible for decades. Understanding ink composition, particle size, and depth allows our specialists to select the most effective laser settings for safe, precise, and successful removal.

Why Tattoo Knowledge Matters for Removal

No two tattoos are the same. Factors such as ink type, pigment composition, tattoo age, application depth, and skin type all affect how a tattoo responds to laser treatment. At ZOE, our deep understanding of tattoo science allows us to create customized treatment plans that maximize results, minimize risks, and deliver a more effective removal experience.
